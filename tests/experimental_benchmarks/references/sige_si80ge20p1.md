## Published properties in this dataset

- Seebeck coefficient (`V*K^(-1)`)
- Electrical conductivity (`ohm^(-1)*m^(-1)`)
- Thermal conductivity (`W*m^(-1)*K^(-1)`)
- Power factor (`W*m^(-1)*K^(-2)`)
- ZT (`-`)
- Lattice thermal conductivity (`W*m^(-1)*K^(-1)`)
- Carrier concentration (`m^(-3)`)
- Carrier mobility (`m^2*V^(-1)*s^(-1)`)
- Electronic thermal conductivity (`W*m^(-1)*K^(-1)`)

The units above are the units **as published** in the StarryData
release and are the units of the raw extract. The processed dataset
converts the two carrier quantities into the conventional
experimental units that its column names declare:

- `published_carrier_concentration_cm3`: published m^-3 divided by 10^6
- `published_carrier_mobility_cm2_Vs`: published m^2/(V.s) multiplied by 10^4
